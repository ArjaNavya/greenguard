Sustainable Plant Care Guide

Master the core principles of watering, sunlight, soil health, and disease prevention.

Smart Watering

Water deeply but less frequently to encourage robust root systems. Check soil moisture 2 inches deep before watering.

Sunlight Exposure

Match plant varieties with optimal sunlight. Most fruiting crops require 6 to 8 hours of direct daily sunlight.

Nutrient-Rich Soil

Maintain balanced pH levels (6.0 - 7.0 for most crops) and enrich soil organically with compost and earthworm humus.

Disease Prevention

Practice crop rotation, sanitize garden pruning tools between cuts, and ensure adequate plant spacing for airflow.
